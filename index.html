01 — Game

Project Solarfly

A turn-based RPG written in C for the Game Boy Advance, set on the inside of a hollow world.

Playable prototype, in development · October 2026

Platform
Game Boy Advance · C · devkitARM
Role
Solo — design and programming
Since
June 2026
  • Positional battles with a shared Speed turn order — twice as fast earns an extra action.
  • Four playable allies with many skills that allow elemental, build-up, tile-based strategy play.
  • Select zooms the camera onto any unit to read its live stats.

02 — Music software

PSP StudiGO

A pocket music studio for the PSP Go — drums, chords and melody played and recorded with nothing but the handheld's buttons.

In development — sessions and piano roll built · October 2026

Platform
Sony PSP Go homebrew · C · PSPSDK
Role
Solo — design and programming
Since
October 2026 (idea notes from 2024)
  • Drums, chords and melody played on the face buttons, d-pad and nub.
  • Eight-track sessions with a piano roll, quantise, undo and redo.
  • A 48-voice engine on its own high-priority thread; exports MIDI and WAV.

A session made in StudiGO

“Session Test” is 8 bars at 72 BPM in E major: a drum groove drawn in the piano roll, a chord part, and two melody lines played on the buttons, saved as a .studigo file. The screen draws that file's real tracks, clips and notes. Press play and the device above follows along.

Audio: rendered with StudiGO's own mixdown function from a PC build of the same engine, not recorded from the device's headphone jack.

About

Hi, I'm Sarp.

I'm a computer science student interested in the joy of using handheld devices and the experiences those tiny physical boxes can bring to users! In my eyes, any device with physical buttons and a small form factor is bound with endless potential, ready to be discovered from inside one's pocket.

Outside of code, I play the piano, mostly coming up with melodic chord progressions and improvising on top of them. I also like exercising, mostly in the form of squash or traditional weight training.

I am open to software engineering and game development opportunities.
