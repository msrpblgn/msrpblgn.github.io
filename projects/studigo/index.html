“Session Test” is 8 bars at 72 BPM in E major: a drum groove drawn in the piano roll, a chord part, and two melody lines played on the buttons, saved as a .studigo file. The screen draws that file's real tracks, clips and notes. The audio was rendered with StudiGO's own mixdown function on a PC build of the same engine, not recorded from the device.

Built and working

  • Three button instruments: a drum kit with six kits and three control layouts, a chord builder that queues a progression and drops each chord held, arpeggiated or staccato, and a melody mode with the eight scale degrees on eight buttons.
  • Sessions of up to eight tracks: record with a count-in, practise along, mute, solo, volume, pan, reverb, delay and clip fades.
  • Editing: split, move, copy and paste clips, duplicate tracks, quantise (1/32 to whole notes, 25–100% strength), undo and redo, and a piano roll.
  • A 44.1 kHz, 48-voice engine: two-operator FM electric piano, saw lead, SFZ sample playback, delay, reverb and a master limiter.
  • Save and load (.studigo), Standard MIDI File export, and WAV mixdown.
  • Host tests for recording timing, edits, undo and file formats, and a host renderer for UI screenshots.

Not built yet

  • Loop region, Looper mode and punch-in.
  • Stem export, microphone sampling, and the coaching / style meter.
