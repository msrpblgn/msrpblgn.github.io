Project Solarfly

A turn-based RPG written in C for the Game Boy Advance, set on the inside of a hollow world.

A 3D model of a teal Game Boy Advance SP. Its screen plays: Joan and Maren on a cliff path. Joan's slash stuns a bat; she walks into it and a battle opens: “Caught off guard! You move first!” Joan and Maren each attack, the log fills in beside the menu, and the party is back on the path.

Built and working

  • A Duncluain story demo: a tutorial spar between Joan and Maren, the forest of Foraois, and a scene at the gate that ends in a boss battle.
  • A party that walks the field together. L/R hand over the lead, and the leader's weapon is the field attack: a slash, a steerable hurricane, a piercing shuriken or a water orb.
  • A Highly Effective field hit stuns a roaming enemy; touch it while it's stunned and the battle opens with it Downed and your side moving first.
  • Turn-based battles on a five-tile formation per side, with targeting, movement skills, interacting status conditions, per-move charges (SC), optional LP costs, and element-versus-kind indicators while you aim.
  • One shared Speed turn order for both teams: a unit at least twice as fast as an opponent gets one extra action just before that opponent's turn.
  • Split View: the command menu shares the bottom panel with a battle log, so messages never hold up the fight.
  • A Select status view: the camera zooms onto any unit and shows its HP, statuses, luck, LP and current stats.
  • A START menu: party and gear, items, Joan's and Maren's skill trees as a turning carousel, an Almanac of elements and enemies, settings, and a cartridge save.
  • Look up (SELECT) in the field: the sky is the far side of the hollow world and the Lamp at its centre, ray traced live as you turn.
  • A 30-stage roguelike mode with reward cards, recruitable allies, two bosses and a battery-backed save.
  • A dialogue system with portraits, cut-ins over battles and authored pacing, plus Tool Demos: a battle builder, a dialogue demo and an overworld test.

Not built yet

  • Connected story maps, NPCs, warps and story flags.
  • Spending points in the skill trees, and real stats for the story demo's gear.
  • A field sprite of Maren's own (she borrows Joan's, tinted blue).
  • The boss's own moves and AI, Conall's Heroism ability, Reika as a recruit, and equipment rewards.
  • Audio and music.

What you're seeing

What I made

  • Game design: the world, characters, battle rules and roguelike structure.
  • The story and the tutorial's script.
  • All engine and gameplay code: battle, scheduler, rendering, field combat, the look-up ray tracer, menus, dialogue, save, tools and host tests.
  • Character sprites, battle backgrounds and the Foraois map, drawn in Aseprite.

Try it

  1. Download the ROM: project_solarfly_gba.gba (Build from 16 Nov 2026 — the same build as the footage on this page)
  2. Install mGBA (0.10 or newer) and open the file with File → Load ROM.
  3. Default keys: X = A, Z = B, arrows = D-pad, Enter = Start, Backspace = Select, A/S = L/R.
  4. On the boot screen, pick Duncluain Story Demo (B skips the tutorial), Rouglike Demo or Tool Demos.
