Procedural Music

A Java model of Western harmony — notes, scales, triad qualities and chord dissonance — built as the base for generating chord progressions.

Harmonic foundation — does not compose yet · May 2025

Language
Java
Role
Solo
Size
One 412-line program
Source on GitHub

Built and working

  • Notes with sharp and flat spellings, octaves and interval distances.
  • Scales built from interval patterns (Major, Minor, Mixolydian). Each degree's triad quality is derived from the pattern, not typed in.
  • Chords from a root, a triad quality (maj, min, dim, sus2, sus4) and any extensions such as ♭9, ♯11 or 13, with omissions.
  • A dissonance score that sums a weight for every interval pair in a chord, and a complexity value per triad quality.
  • Note-to-triad mapping: an out-of-scale note borrows the quality of the degree just below or just above it, chosen at random.

Not built yet

  • Choosing the next chord — progressions and transitions.
  • Rhythm, voicing and voice leading.
  • Audio or MIDI output, and tests.

What I made

  • All of it: the music-theory model, the scoring values and the code.
